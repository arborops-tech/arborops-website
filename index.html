Real problems. Real solutions. Every system below addresses a specific bottleneck we've seen SMEs lose money to — every single day.

Lead Capture

24/7 WhatsApp Lead Qualification & Booking Agent

The Bottleneck

Potential clients enquire after hours, on weekends, or during your busiest periods. No reply means a lost lead — often to a competitor who responds first.

The AI Solution

An intelligent WhatsApp agent that understands natural language — including Hinglish and Kanglish — answers your FAQs, qualifies leads based on budget and intent, and books appointments directly into your calendar. No human needed.

◆ Stops leads from falling through the cracks. Measurable impact on top-line revenue from week one.
Brand Protection

Omnichannel Reputation & Review Manager

The Bottleneck

Replying to Google, Zomato, or Trustpilot reviews takes time you don't have. Ignoring negative reviews costs you customers. Ignoring positive ones costs you loyalty.

The AI Solution

A sentiment analysis dashboard that aggregates reviews across every platform, instantly alerts you to negative reviews via WhatsApp, and auto-drafts empathetic, context-aware responses — ready for your one-click approval.

◆ Protects your brand's reputation at scale — without hiring a social media manager.
Repeat Revenue

Hyper-Personalised Customer Re-engagement Engine

The Bottleneck

You're sitting on a goldmine of past customer data but only sending generic promotional blasts that get ignored. Low open rates, low conversions, high unsubscribes.

The AI Solution

An AI tool that segments your customer database and automatically drafts highly personalised SMS or email campaigns based on past purchase behaviour — "Hey Priya, it's been 3 months since you bought [Product X]. Ready for a refill?"

◆ Drives repeat business with zero extra effort from you. Your data, finally working for you.
